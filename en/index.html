Your pace, your path.

See your changes. Find your pace.

LumaWeight is a read-only Apple Health weight-trend app for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. No account is required.

Get started

  1. Choose Connect Apple Health and select the data you wish to allow in the system permission sheet.
  2. Explore Today, Trends and Calendar, or choose Try sample first to see synthesized records.
  3. LumaWeight does not write Health data. Add or edit measurements in Apple Health, then refresh LumaWeight.

No records appear

Confirm that Apple Health contains weight records under Body Measurements → Weight, and check LumaWeight’s read permissions in Health settings. Open Settings → Weight sync details to inspect the state and reconnect.

An empty result can mean no records or no read access; the app cannot distinguish those cases. A failed refresh preserves the previous result and shows its status.

Trends and recording rhythm

Trends starts with 30 days and also supports 7 days, 90 days, a year and all history. Daily trends use the last reading each day. The 7-day average uses available measurements without filling missing days. Longer periods use labeled weekly or monthly aggregation.

Recording rhythm counts unique days. There is no requirement to weigh daily. Samples show an overall weight-loss journey; real charts always follow your actual measurements.

Journey, units and reminders

Choose a real starting point and target in Journey. End a stage to keep a local review. Settings supports kg, jin and lb, optional height, and optional reads of existing body measurements. Reminders are local notifications you enable and can turn off.

Targets and BMI are personal-record displays, not diagnosis, treatment or personalized weight-loss advice.

Sharing and widgets

Inspect images before sharing. The 30-day share view can hide absolute weight with Show change only; dates and changes may still be personal information. Widgets use a local snapshot. Refresh the main app first; the system controls final widget update timing.

Permissions and deletion

Revoke access in Apple Health or system privacy settings. Revoking access does not automatically remove saved stage reviews. Use Delete App in iOS to remove local application data; Offload App may retain documents and data. Manage original Health records, exported images and system backups separately.

Contact support

Use GitHub Issues and include the app version, device model, iOS version and reproduction steps.

Issues are public. Do not post health records, phone numbers, passwords or other sensitive details. Redact personal information from screenshots.
