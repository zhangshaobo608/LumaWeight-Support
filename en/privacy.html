Privacy policy

This policy covers the LumaWeight iOS app and its widget, provided by Shenzhen Huoli Zhihui Technology Co., Ltd.

No app data collection

LumaWeight requires no account and contains no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDK. We do not receive, upload or sell your health records, stage targets, preferences or usage activity, and do not maintain server-side personal profiles.

Health access and local processing

With your permission, the app reads weight, timestamps and sources from Apple Health for trends, calendar views and reviews. If you choose to inspect body measurements, you can separately authorize reads of height, body-fat percentage, BMI, lean body mass and waist circumference. The app does not add, change or delete Apple Health records.

Calculations run on your device. Stage targets, completed-stage record snapshots, manually entered height, units and reminder settings are stored locally. The widget reads a recent weight snapshot and display settings through the app’s own shared container, not containers belonging to other developers. There is no cloud account or app-initiated cloud synchronization; device and system backups are managed through your system settings.

Sharing and optional permissions

Images leave the app only when you use system sharing or save them to Photos. Weight, changes and dates may be personal information: inspect the preview before sharing. Services you choose as recipients have their own policies.

Notification permission is for local reminders, with no remote push or advertising. Photo-library add permission is used only when you choose to save a review image.

Control, retention and deletion

Manage Health and notification permissions in Apple Health or system settings. Revoking Health access does not automatically erase saved local stage snapshots. To remove local application data, use Delete App in iOS; Offload App may retain data. Original Health records remain in Apple Health. Manage shared copies, Photos, recipient copies and system backups separately.

Website and support

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and security logs under its privacy statement. The app does not attach your health information when opening these pages.

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, we receive what you choose to submit and it may be public. Do not submit raw health records, passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use our support channel without including sensitive data in public posts. We will update this page and relevant app information if our practices change.
